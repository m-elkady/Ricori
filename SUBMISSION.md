# Submission

Include the lockfile, migrations, tests and updated startup instructions. Exclude dependencies, compiled output, database data, credentials and local logs.

Include a concise `HANDOVER.md` covering:

1. Your causal explanation of the behaviors you investigated, including any relationship between reports.
2. What you changed, why it is sufficient, and how ordinary workflows are preserved.
3. Exact checks executed and their actual results. Separate observed results from proposed verification.
4. Remaining issues, assumptions, tradeoffs and useful next steps.
5. Meaningful tool or AI assistance and how you verified its output.

Partial solutions are acceptable. Clearly distinguish implemented corrections from plans. Extra features and large rewrites do not increase the score. Commit history is helpful but not required.
